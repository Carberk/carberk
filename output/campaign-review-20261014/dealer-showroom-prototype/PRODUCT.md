# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Carberk buyers, dealership owners and sales staff. This browser artifact simulates the agreed mobile journeys.
## Product purpose
Explore one app with personal and dealership workspaces, business-owned inventory and enquiries, and public listing/showroom sharing.
## Capabilities and constraints
Free dealer access. Personal logins. Owner controls members; staff publish and maintain stock. Explicit seller context. Draft, Active, Sold, Archived. Manual enquiry assignment. Weekly availability review without ranking boosts. Public web fallbacks for shared listings and showrooms. Sold pages preserve sold status; withdrawn pages hide listing content. Synthetic data, memory only; no production accounts, sends, API calls or deployments. Invitations, domain, language routing and business verification evidence remain open. This is not an approved canonical specification.
## Brand commitments
Public name Carberk. Preserve incumbent red brand and neutral palette; existing Geist font and repository car fixtures.
## Evidence on hand
Repository source facts and /tmp/carberk-dealer-showroom-decisions-20261014.md. All business and staff names and inventory details in this artifact are fictional.
