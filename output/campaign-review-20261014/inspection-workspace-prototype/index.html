Cars are inspected at the center. One service plan. Price and mechanical scope await approval.

This prototype explores the full center workflow, from intake to a reviewed private or public report. All vehicles, people and findings are fictional. Role switches and access checks are simulations.

Prototype assumptions and limits

Separate reviewer required in this demo. All checks and panels need an explicit result or a reason they were not checked. Optional evidence can be omitted with an explanation. These are reviewable proposals, not approved operating policy. Memory only; refreshing clears changes. No bookings, payments, uploads, messages or reports leave this browser. English workspace with draft Turkmen checklist wording. No real authentication or production PDF generation.
