Boundaries for review

Call intent is deduplicated per measured account/guest installation, Listing and UTC day. Chat starts are new Conversations. The inventory denominator accounts for active time. Neither is a sale. Accounts and guest installations are separate measurement units, never a summed count of people. Small groups are combined; that alone does not prove anonymization. Raw content, phone/email, exact location and replay are absent. Reviewer/test and known owner interactions are excluded from conversion examples. Production event ownership, permissions, retention, lawful delivery, late-event completeness and operational thresholds need their approved contracts and proof.

Proposals shown here: three product tabs, evidence drawer, seven completed UTC days by default, stale-data comparison guard, investigation reason, and fixes linked to the existing issue workflow. Language shown is English for this walkthrough. Proposed initial production labels are Russian: Обзор, Пути и возвраты, Надёжность и исправления. Q45 remains pending.
