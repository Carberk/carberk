## Final policy resolutions

### Dealer operating policy

Founder approval admits identifiable car-selling businesses. One User belongs to one business initially. A protected owner manages invitations, staff removal and verified business contact proof; recovery is manual and audited. Current members can read business enquiries, sales staff claim them and the owner reassigns. Removal revokes access and returns assignments to Unassigned while preserving business inventory/history. Business suspension hides public inventory/showroom and prevents new work. Personal-to-business transfers are deferred. Business verification makes no vehicle-condition claim.

### Diagnostic authority and presentation

Assigned technicians author findings. A different qualified reviewer releases public reports; private reports may initially use audited self-review. Physical vehicle authorization and public publication consent are separate prerequisites. Public identifiers are masked, corrections preserve prior versions, and material condition changes require reinspection. Identity/ownership changes require revalidation before public association. Reports show the observed date/mileage without a guaranteed validity period. The reader may be released with a clearly pending/retryable PDF.

An available authorized public report produces a neutral localized **Inspection report available** badge on Results cards and Listing detail. It opens complete findings with inspection date/mileage. It must not imply a pass, guarantee, condition grade or ranking boost. No green approval checkmark. Withdrawal/removal or unavailable access must not leave a misleading available badge. Buyer-private reports never produce public metadata, badges or existence signals, and are not converted or resold publicly.

### Service failures

Missing a paid Vehicle History delivery promise immediately escalates the case. One additional staffed hour requires customer agreement; refusal or another miss goes to the founder's refund decision. Approval and actual refund execution remain separate states. Diagnostic service failures get correction/reinspection at company cost or a founder refund decision, without selective deletion of findings.

The daily service schedule in Asia/Ashgabat and weekday incident-response coverage in Asia/Shanghai are separate commitments. Preserve both rather than silently replacing one with the other.

### Every admin area supports three languages

Turkmen, Russian and English are required throughout the admin shell, analytics/Improvement, dealer management, Support, Vehicle History and inspection workspaces. This supersedes the earlier Russian-only initial Improvement proposal. Include navigation, charts, filters, forms, validation, access/error/loading/empty states and generated user-facing reports/exports. Preserve original provider artifacts and technical identifiers rather than inventing translated source evidence.

### Durable vehicle knowledge and anonymized insights

The business goal is a lasting Turkmenistan vehicle/market dataset, including lawful monetization and sale of anonymized insights. Keep useful permitted vehicle facts and genuinely non-identifying derived market knowledge indefinitely where allowed, otherwise for the longest justified permitted period. The proposed blanket 90-day service/24-month inspection purge was rejected. This does not authorize indefinite retention or sale of all personal/private/provider-controlled records.

Useful demographic analysis may include age range and coarse city/region. Prefer purposeful optional self-reporting; distinguish residence from search market and Listing location, and preserve unknown/uncertain provenance. Names are unnecessary. Exact birth dates, addresses and continuous precise location are not justified merely to compute those insights. Do not infer demographics from private messages or photos.

Account-linked fields remain personal without names. Removing names, hashing identifiers or retaining stable VINs does not establish anonymity. Separate personal records, buyer-private inspection evidence and third-party artifacts from durable commercial aggregates. Record source provenance, permitted purposes and redistribution rights. Prevent identifying small groups through combined filters; evaluate anonymization against realistic auxiliary data. Personal deletion, source restrictions and required rights remain effective. Existing bounded raw telemetry schedules are not silently replaced by the business-data goal.

The campaign delivers data foundations and internal market analysis. A data-sales portal, an actual commercial disclosure and raw customer/contact-data sales are outside this delegation.
