## Why widening the trigger is insufficient

1. [ExpireServiceContent.ts](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/ExpireServiceContent.ts#L19) globally selects closed cases, then at lines20–25 takes case/conversation locks before any campaign namespace admission. Its constructor accepts only Prisma and storage. There is no serving-candidate check, reservation classifier or lifecycle authority call. The first rejected write at line30 changes `erasedAt`/epoch. Opening that shape alone would allow the global worker to begin changing an owned graph without the required current full candidate and reservation authority.
2. The worker commits erasure and a durable fence at lines30–41, clearing `SupportCase.customerId` and `assigneeId` at line38. [ServiceErasureFence](../../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L1342) retains only conversationId, epoch, objectKeys, erasedAt and cleanupCompletedAt, with a **Restrict** conversation FK. It has no direct roster email or reservation/candidate binding. A later process must resolve a pending fence's ownership after nulling, without trusting stale selected work or treating unresolved campaign data as ordinary work. Existing journal keys retain ownership, but no worker resolver exposes that authority.
3. At worker lines73–78 a separate global pending sweep performs storage I/O **before** the conditional completion UPDATE, outside a transaction or namespace fence. Its epoch/manifest predicate protects that UPDATE, but supplies no namespace serialization, current candidate proof or live journal hash proof before storage. Reset can interleave with an already-selected cleanup. Completing the fence by merely opening another trigger shape does not fix that race.
4. [S3RetiredObjectStore.ts](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/S3RetiredObjectStore.ts#L29) checks bucket versioning even for `[]`. It has no DB dependency or reservation identity. Keep it unchanged. Moving/skipping its empty-manifest check would weaken the unchanged acceptance case; a safe cleanup wrapper must surround the real call and recheck exact fence authority.
5. [withCampaignEmailDelivery](../../../packages/db/src/campaign-email-delivery.ts#L13) is a useful callback/fence pattern, but its caller always supplies a durable roster email and exact requestId. It derives one fixed-grammar namespace and proves the ledger under that fence. Service cleanup has no corresponding durable principal input after nulling. Reusing this helper by borrowing an arbitrary retained audit actor or by falling back on a guessed namespace would introduce a new ownership rule. The existing [API admission](../../../apps/api/src/modules/service-messaging/infrastructure/campaignServiceAdmission.ts#L23) likewise needs a live principal/Session and is inappropriate for a90-day background purge. Do not bypass Session expiry by passing fake principals or null arguments to it.
6. [staging-campaign.mjs](../../../scripts/staging-campaign.mjs#L250) tracks six service tables, not `service_erasure_fences`; its key logic defaults to `{id}` except staff grants and read state. The fence needs exact `{conversationId}` across tracking/integrity/preflight/reset. The service authority is principal/Session-based, not lifecycle-based. Existing trigger `OLD` hash validation and foreign-parent checks can be reused only after the worker authority boundary exists. ## Decision to carry to root

Do not relax service write shapes in this attempt. The dispatch permits this stop and does not authorize inventing a restart-safe worker resource resolver or ambient ownership protocol.

The smallest next design must settle one question: **how does a worker resolve an exact case/conversation/fence to its unique reservation after customer/assignee nulling and across process restart, while distinguishing ordinary work from unresolved/refused campaign work?** Direct client namespaces, in-memory-only maps, fabricated Sessions, `mode='synthetic'` alone and a mutable actor's email alone are insufficient. A schema change is not asserted necessary. Namespace-local journal metadata or a server-owned resolver over exact journal keys may be feasible, but their discovery/ambiguity/failure semantics are a new authority interface and must be selected explicitly before implementation. This note does not choose a new schema, partition or protocol.

Once that resolver is selected, the required bounded implementation is concrete:

- Before case row locks, acquire the affected tables in reset-compatible order, then the derived namespace fence. Recheck exact resource/current full serving candidate/full-v3 identities state, contract/schema/journal/event hashes, current case/conversation OLD hashes and due closedAt, then prove an owned text-only graph with no foreign edges, attachments or subscriptions. Reread after the fence; a reset/deletion or reopened case causes no work, never adoption.
- Admit only the exact purge transition: epoch+1/erasedAt, owned message/outbox/read-state deletion, reason-only existing-audit updates, exact case linkage clearing/version+1, null-actor SERVICE_CONTENT_PURGED with original resource/version, and an exact empty-manifest fence. Trigger exceptions must prove this lifecycle authority and OLD/NEW hashes, not an arbitrary setting/token. Preserve ordinary processing, retention clocks, selectors' limits, transaction timeouts and concurrency.
- Resolve and serialize pending cleanup again before storage. Require the same current lifecycle binding, live exact journal hash, epoch and `[]` manifest; call real storage under that boundary and admit only null-to-completed completion of that exact fence. No History, private bytes or general attachment authority follows.
- Extend journal composite-key handling, inventory, foreign-reference checks and locked reset order for the fence. Reset only after exact completed-empty proof, delete it before conversation, preserve retained redacted audit IDs, and prove worker/reset retries and late-selected work cannot resurrect rows. No job cleanup or operational entry opening.

The unchanged hosted test is necessary but does not prove changed-candidate, foreign due closed-case or already-selected reset/reopen races. The next boundary tests should exercise the real worker/resource authority with fakes only at DB/storage/time boundaries. No repository module mocks or guard-string mirrors were added here.
