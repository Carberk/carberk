## Ordinary sequence and authority

Required behavior comes from [feature 50](../../prd/features/50-reports-tier.md#failure-and-acceptance-criteria), [flow 66](../../prd/flows/66-diagnostic-inspection.md#failure-and-recovery-examples), [PDF acceptance](../../prd/features/51-pdf-export.md#acceptance-criteria) and [ADR-0104](../../adr/0104-center-diagnostics-with-private-and-public-reports.md). Source establishes what can run today. The [roster](../../../scripts/staging-campaign.mjs#L237) contains the aliases below; `outsider` is not an alias.

| Exact alias | Required authority and use |
|---|---|
| `coordinator` | Existing bootstrap `staff.manage`; ordinary email OTP, first TOTP enrollment and elevation; HTTP grant each capability below with reason. No self-grant, Marketplace Role promotion, signed JWT or inserted elevated Session. |
| `reception-steward` | `diagnostics.reception`; propose also `diagnostics.templates` on this same alias. Source supports that combination; root must settle this allocation, which the matrix leaves unnamed. Needs both center qualifications. |
| `technician` | `diagnostics.technician`, qualified at the center and assigned to the public order. Authors v1 and correction v2. |
| `independent-reviewer` | `diagnostics.reviewer`, qualified and assigned to the public order; different User from its author. |
| `private-reviewer` | Both `diagnostics.technician` and `diagnostics.reviewer`, with both center qualifications and both private-order assignments. Authors then explicitly self-reviews with `privateSelfReview=true` and nonempty reason. No separate self-review capability exists. |
| `owner` | Ordinary commissioner of the personal public Listing inspection; physical authorization and publication consent remain distinct recorded authorities. Seller has no automatic private-report access. |
| `buyer-a` | Ordinary private commissioner and reader. Choosing not to buy changes no audience. |
| `buyer-b` | Ordinary unrelated buyer for protected private reader/evidence/PDF refusal; public mobile reader. Anonymous public web requests cover signed-out outsiders without creating another alias. |

Staff grants, center qualifications and order assignments are three different checks. [DiagnosticAuthority](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/DiagnosticAuthority.ts#L16) checks current identity, current elevated staff authority and unrevoked/unexpired center qualification; order staff access additionally needs an unrevoked assignment. `diagnostics.templates` alone does not grant report access. [Reception assigns order capabilities](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/PrismaDiagnosticIntake.ts#L191), but cannot create center qualifications. Private self-review uses the [ordinary reviewer transition](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/PrismaDiagnosticReports.ts#L126) and [audience separation rule](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/domain/DiagnosticReport.ts#L94).

Minimum dependency order, after an approved owned synthetic center bootstrap:

1. Sign in coordinator and participating staff through email OTP delivery; enroll/elevate via normal TOTP; coordinator grants the exact capabilities. Sign in participating customers normally. Keep Session/grant/OTP/TOTP rows in this reservation's journal. Apply S2 removal/stale-elevation denials to the relevant actors without editing their expiry timestamps.
2. Reception publishes template v1 through HTTP. Owner creates public intake against an ordinary owned active Listing; buyer-a creates a distinct private intake. Retry the same commissioner/request key and read back the same order, not another appointment. A changed payload conflicts. Intake auto-assigns qualified reception staff. Requested is not confirmed.
3. Reception records physical authorization on each order, separate publication authorization only on the public order, confirms then marks arrived, and assigns the technician/reviewer capabilities. Technician records the physical observation and public Listing association. No fabricated observation time or qualification deadline.
4. Public technician creates draft, saves every required checklist/body assessment and evidence, exercises upload retry with stable finding/evidence IDs, and submits. Independent reviewer returns with reason where required, then finalizes complete v1. Reader can release while PDFs remain pending. Compare TK/RU/EN PDFs with this immutable snapshot after readiness.
5. Publish template v2, prove v1 and its order stay pinned. Correct public v1 into linked v2 with the same original observation date/mileage and template; change findings, submit, separately review, finalize. Keep v1 and all six locale artifacts. Withdraw current v2; Results/detail badge and public reads disappear without falling back to v1. Authorized history and original audience remain.
6. Private-reviewer authors and submits buyer-a's private report, then uses explicit audited self-review. Buyer-a reads it and its TK/RU/EN PDFs. Owner, buyer-b, anonymous routes, unassigned staff and removed staff cannot obtain private reader, evidence, PDF or public existence metadata. Capture dirty-work/reopen/map-list equivalence, denied/stale/revoked reads, failed evidence/PDF retry and locale coverage under the same snapshot/IDs.

These are required journeys, not a claim that the campaign can execute them. [Runtime](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/diagnosticRuntime.ts#L6) requires test/staging, explicit synthetic user IDs, and a separate public gate. [Availability is always false](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/presentation/DiagnosticIntakeController.ts#L37). The full-v3 [table list/parent rules](../../../scripts/staging-campaign.mjs#L243) do not own diagnostics. Merely composing DiagnosticsModule could let unjournaled writes escape; an HTTP success would not prove ownership or safe reset.

## Owned keys, edges and disposal

Every diagnostics row below uses journal key `{id}`. Composite uniqueness is a collision barrier as well as the primary-key barrier. Only exact journal membership conveys ownership; actor identity or a matching center code alone cannot adopt existing data. [Schema](../../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L1354), [reports/evidence/artifacts](../../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L1669).

| Table | Exact owning/foreign edges and unique selectors | Proposed synthetic reset disposition |
|---|---|---|
| `diagnostic_centers` | Root id; globally unique `code`, default `ashgabat`; no User ownership edge | New reservation-owned center only; never adopt the default center or overwrite operational approval. Remove last. |
| `diagnostic_center_assignments` | `centerId` → owned center, `userId` → exact roster; unique `(centerId,userId,capability)` | Exact qualification tuples only; remove before center/users. Deadline/provenance policy needs approval. |
| `diagnostic_template_versions` | `centerId` → owned center; nullable `publishedById` → roster; unique `(centerId,version)` | Preserve published versions during acceptance; delete after reports/orders. Never select latest across centers. |
| `inspection_orders` | `centerId`, `templateVersionId` → same owned center; nullable `commissionerId` → roster, `listingId` → owned Listing; unique `(commissionerId,requestKey)` | Fence access/epoch before storage cleanup; delete after report and audit children. Public Listing is an explicit prerequisite, never adopted by seller-id matching. |
| `diagnostic_order_assignments` | `orderId` → owned order, `userId` → roster; unique `(orderId,userId,capability)` | Retain revoked tuples in budget; delete exact keys. |
| `diagnostic_audits` | `centerId`, nullable `orderId`, nullable `actorId`; `details` embeds template/report/finding/predecessor/target-user IDs and reasons | Proposed exact deletion after exporting redacted acceptance evidence. Existing retained `audit_logs` policy does not automatically cover this separate table; root must approve disposition. Its RESTRICT edges otherwise prevent order/center deletion. |
| `diagnostic_reports` | `orderId`, `templateVersionId`; nullable unique `predecessorId` → same-order/audience predecessor; JSON state/snapshot contains author/reviewer, finding/evidence IDs | Preserve immutable snapshots throughout acceptance. Synthetic disposal must remove successors before predecessors, after evidence/artifacts. No rewriting snapshots to make reset pass. |
| `diagnostic_evidence` | `reportId`; JSON finding references may come from any same-order predecessor/successor; `createdById` and `findingId` have no schema FK. Unique `objectKey` | Exact private manifest, hash/size/mime/epoch/write and cleanup leases. Keep referenced predecessor evidence. Delete rows only after verified byte disposal and late-writer barrier. |
| `diagnostic_pdf_artifacts` | `reportId`, `snapshotHash`; unique `(reportId,locale,rendererVersion,generation)` and `objectKey` | Three intents per release. Exact hash/size/epoch/generation/lease disposal before report deletion; no prefix deletion. |

Private bucket is restricted to `diagnostic-private` or `diagnostic-test-private` in [API storage](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/S3DiagnosticObjects.ts#L20) and [worker storage](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/diagnostics/S3DiagnosticArtifactObjects.ts#L15). Evidence key is `diagnostic-evidence/<evidenceId>/<sanitized-sha256>`; PDF key is `diagnostic-pdf/<reportId>/<artifactId>.pdf`. [Evidence intent](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/PrismaDiagnosticEvidence.ts#L29) commits before I/O; matching verified retry adds no row, conflicting content/ref binding refuses. [Documents](../../../apps/api/src/modules/diagnostics/infrastructure/PrismaDiagnosticDocuments.ts#L50) reauthorize after I/O and compare artifact identity/epoch/hash/size. Public evidence must also be referenced in the released findings.

Diagnostics has no separate service-message outbox. PDF artifact rows are the durable work intents. [Worker composition](../../../apps/worker/src/diagnostic-artifacts.module.ts) schedules a recurring sweep; [execute/cleanup](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/diagnostics/DiagnosticArtifactWorker.ts#L119) select globally across pending/failed/expired-processing artifacts, erased reports and stale/unattached evidence. Stopping one known Bull job does not fence that database sweep. Existing [worker tests](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/diagnostics/DiagnosticArtifactWorker.e2e.spec.ts) cover storage/late-writer behavior, not reservation ownership.

Reset/retry needs all of these barriers before the graph can be admitted:

- Bind reservation/profile/candidate/schema hash, manifest and journal hashes, exact row-key versions and table locks to the current candidate. Extend existing [foreign-reference scan and locking](../../../scripts/staging-campaign.mjs#L436), including external schemas. A foreign child, changed row, reused key/code/request key/artifact key, mixed reservation parent, or report JSON reference outside the graph refuses before deletion. Catalog FKs are shared read references and convey no ownership.
- Add explicit diagnostics tracking and disposal rules; do not allow generic actor-based adoption. Validate center/template/order/audience consistency and all JSON evidence/author/reviewer/predecessor references. Existing schema [pinning triggers](../../../packages/db/prisma/migrations/20261015190000_diagnostic_reports/migration.sql#L122) are additional invariants, not ownership checks.
- Fence new admissions and worker claims for the reservation first. Mark exact artifact/evidence cleanup intents and change epochs while retaining active write/generation leases. Wait for bounded lease completion; delete exact objects and verify absence under the storage adapter's versioning checks. Preserve durable cleanup intents/tombstones until a resumed writer cannot recreate bytes. Worker cleanup deliberately rechecks completed intents; deleting their rows early removes that protection. A permanent reservation-aware worker fence/drain proof is still missing.
- Resume reset after lost response by manifest/row/intent readback, not another bootstrap. Order retry uses `(commissionerId,requestKey)`; correction retry uses unique predecessor plus successor-id/reason/author equality. Template publication has only expected-version conflict, not request-key replay: read `(centerId,version)` and audit before deciding whether the lost response committed. Finalize retry must read the released snapshot/artifact tuple instead of finalizing again. Cleanup network failure remains unfinished, never a successful reset.

Production retention is different from synthetic fixture disposal. [Feature 50's classified policy](../../prd/features/50-reports-tier.md#final-discovery-and-classified-data-policy) sets no blanket report purge duration. [Account erasure](../../../apps/worker/src/jobs/diagnosticAccountErasure.ts#L7) fences artifacts/evidence before unlinking intake, removes personal attribution and preserves protected snapshots. Withdrawal hides public access; it does not erase historical reports or bytes. Synthetic deletion and diagnostic-audit disposition above remain design decisions, not permission to purge real observations. Keep campaign audit redaction and permanent OTP delivery tombstones unchanged.

## Literal budget, fixed 128 records / 2048 events

The [inventory accounting rules](full-v3-capacity-inventory-20261020.md#accounting-rules-source-at-base) apply: deleted keys still count; updates/deletes are events; OTP deliveries have a separate fixed 128 cap. No guessed slack. Let `S` be ordinary sign-ins, `K` first TOTP enrollments including coordinator, `G` distinct nonbootstrap capability tuples, and `U` the unresolved OTP verification update events. Authentication costs **21 + 2S + 11K + 2G records**, **21 + 2S + 13K + 2G + U events** before refresh/revocation/reset, and `S` deliveries. Extra device sign-ins cost two records and one delivery each.

These are literal lower bounds for the sequence above, conditional on the proposed alias allocation, not approved quotas. `F` counts successful finding saves, including correction edits; `E` counts distinct evidence intents including failed/abandoned uploads. Actual F/E are UNKNOWN until the validated template and failure schedule are fixed. At least one public evidence retry requires E ≥ 1; body/check completeness can require more.
