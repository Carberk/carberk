with no partial effects, truncation, smaller-command retry or skipped roots.

over-specific fixture assertion, not a product requirement to admit that graph.
Ordinary append prepares the order's retained graph and already enforces the
1,000-key bound. Do not weaken that admission guard to manufacture a fixture.

The coordinator may separately dispatch a correction to the `overlimit` case
using complete, lawfully produced ordinary roots whose connected prepared union
exceeds 1,000 keys in one actual worker command. Use shared retained edges and
record them. At most ten roots may occupy the unchanged first production group.
The total of several individually under-cap commands does not qualify. A raw-row
count is not the graph-key count; include the complete registered dependencies
and derived effects. Prove cardinality pressure rather than relying only on an
exception that also covers the iteration bound.

Retain actual restricted-worker execution, the exact
`diagnostic_source_scope_limit` refusal, whole persisted-state equality, zero
source/campaign receipts and restoration. Put the failing group first so the
existing all-row rollback assertion remains meaningful. Keep foreign controls,
the independent component, selection rules and unchanged production width,
100/1,000/iteration limits, budgets and rights horizon. No protected-row inserts,
pre-guard fixture lane, cap increase or production partition change is authorized.

A connected singleton above the cap must still refuse if encountered. This
decision does not claim a singleton runtime proof or permit subdivision of that
root. The impossible one-order/raw-count shape is superseded by the complete
connected command-union criterion above. If that lawful fixture cannot be
produced within the retained limits, report the exact dependency and keep the
case open.
