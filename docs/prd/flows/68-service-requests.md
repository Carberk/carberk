# 68 - Business assistance and vehicle-history requests

## Vehicle History journey

1. User opens compact Home entry, Listing-detail entry or request history in Messages. Public explanation states 100 TMT buys available Carfax/AutoCheck history, either alone sufficient, both when obtainable; authorized sample is downloadable. It promises neither fresh data nor complete/local coverage.
2. Sign-in is required to contact staff. New request may be questions without a VIN. Returning user can open previous reports or begin a new request. No enquiry creates a payment, slot reservation or paid lookup by itself.
3. Authorized staff claims request. User/operator establish exact VIN and optional source Listing. Check input/product/identity/availability through approved mechanism; unsupported/no-data/provider outage/mismatch remain distinct and withhold payment instructions.
4. Operator proposes 100 TMT and available-provider offer with exact VIN, scheduled day/window and refund rules. User explicitly confirms. Operator chooses permitted archive or new lookup; archive date is not shown before payment, original date remains in delivered artifact and no freshness claim is made.
5. Payment instructions reserve accepted day for two hours within today/next-day bookable capacity. Notice explains outside-hours verification. User submits receipt screenshot or sender phone. Assigned operator verifies funds; image/claim alone cannot confirm. Expiry releases unpaid hold; late/uncertain payment enters reconciliation with a newly agreed feasible day or founder refund decision.
6. Verified paid orders enter oldest-confirmed-payment-first queue. Assigned operator fulfils using one fenced order/purchase intent. Uncertain purchase result is reconciled before retry; explicit handover removes old owner's mutation authority. Provider/operation capacity and closing/morning commitments are enforced separately.
7. Operator checks exact confirmed VIN, provider/date and usable PDF/pages and link, then associates original artifacts and delivers. One provider is sufficient; neither is failure. User opens/downloads/shares under permissions. Reopen/retry does not buy another report. Prior customer chat/payment data never appears in archive reuse.
8. At missed promised time immediately escalate. Only explicit user agreement grants one extra staffed hour with a recorded deadline. Refusal or second miss goes to founder refund decision. Operator error wrong VIN is corrected at company cost or refunded if not possible; user-confirmed wrong car after purchase requires a new purchase. Refund approval and execution are separately visible facts.
9. Completion leaves request/report history under applicable personal/provider rights. It creates permitted provenance-rich archive knowledge, not unrestricted ownership of customer/payment/provider content. Personal deletion follows category policy and holds; useful permitted knowledge can outlive the purchaser association where legally/contractually allowed.

Daily service hours are 09:00-20:00 Asia/Ashgabat. Normal delivery is within one staffed hour when feasible. Today is bookable until closing only when achievable; accepted next-day prepaid batch must fit measured delivery by 10:00. Full today and tomorrow stops payment instructions, not enquiries. No silent promise change or unlimited paid backlog.

## General Support journey

Messages Support row and Cabinet Help reach the same Support history. Signed-out Help retains contact details and sign-in. A restricted user can appeal. Staff replies carry business identity; private notes and operator audit are distinct from public messages. Text/one compressed <=5 MB image only. No provider order/payment or general PDF/broadcast flow is implied.

A meaningful staff reply answers the oldest unanswered user-message clock. Manual closure is allowed when resolved or after notice and 14 days waiting for the user; never while staff owes investigation/reply. New message reopens within retained history; after purge it starts fresh. Preserve the accepted 90-day-after-closure and account-deletion content policy. Daily Ashgabat coverage/four staffed-hour target belongs to Support, while incident handling uses its separate process.

## Failure and access invariants

Every read, file URL, message, claim, payment verification, delivery and export is authorized server-side for purpose/object/current actor state. Signed-out, non-purchaser, wrong service capability, previous assignee, disabled staff and unauthorized dealer/seller cannot retrieve private data through known IDs. Customer access does not disappear merely because staff ownership changes. Static prototype role switches are not permission evidence.

Draft/pending/verified/fulfilled and failed/refund states must not collapse into one successful status. Invalid state changes return actionable localized conflicts without side effects; refresh reflects authoritative state. Retry does not duplicate messages, receipt confirmation, capacity or provider purchases. Keep original documents unchanged and render technical identifiers safely. All user/operator states and generated exports support TK/RU/EN; original provider documents need no invented translation.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Questions without VIN, return to prior reports and duplicate VIN warning without automatic repurchase.
- One/both/no provider, archived artifact reuse with correct provenance and private prior-order isolation.
- Quote acceptance, two-hour hold race, late receipt, after-hours verification and full today/next day.
- FIFO and capacity versus actual morning staffing, near-closing agreed next-day scheduling.
- Visible claim/handover and previous operator refused at direct mutation endpoint.
- Invalid/wrong-VIN PDF, failed download/link, refused delay, agreed recovery and re-miss, pending/completed refund.
- Support restricted-user appeal, image upload/send retry, meaningful reply versus note, close/reopen and purge/account deletion.
- Unauthorized customer/staff artifact/exports, disabled capabilities and permitted archive retention without accidental personal retention.
