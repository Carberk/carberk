# 53 - Vehicle History service

## Purpose and boundary

Carberk sells available foreign vehicle history for imported vehicles and preserves permitted source knowledge in an internal archive. This is a human-operated service, distinct from General Support, Listing Conversations, physical diagnosis and InspectionInterest. The service can handle a Carberk Listing or an outside vehicle. It makes no claim of Turkmenistan record coverage, complete history, authentic physical VIN or mechanical condition.

[ADR-0106](../../adr/0106-service-owned-support-and-vehicle-history.md) governs ownership and rights boundaries. The [service flow](../flows/68-service-requests.md) owns the end-to-end journey and [workspace design](../ui/service-workspace.md) owns visible states. This is agreed target behavior, not evidence it is implemented. Live provider/payment activation remains disabled until the gates below pass.

## Offer and discovery

100 TMT buys available Carfax or AutoCheck history. Provide both when obtainable; one provider alone satisfies the provider-count requirement. State that availability rule before payment. A delivered provider report includes a usable downloadable PDF and shared link. The link supplements the PDF obligation, not a substitute. Preserve source disclaimers and original report date. Do not merge conflicting source findings into a Carberk verdict or treat missing records as proof of no accident.

Assigned operator chooses a permitted archived artifact or a new lookup. The customer does not choose cached versus fresh. Under the final founder choice, do not display the archive date before payment; retain it unaltered in the delivered artifact and internal archive. Standard offer and repeat actions must not promise fresh/current/updated history. Reusing a permitted artifact can cost 100 TMT. Reopening/downloading the same purchased report creates no new order or charge.

A compact Vehicle History entry sits above brand/model selection; Listing detail also has an entry. Messages holds request history. Public information, 100 TMT price and authorized educational sample are readable before sign-in; contacting an operator requires sign-in. Returning users see prior requests and New request. Same-VIN repeat warning offers Open previous reports and New request. Public Listing report publication and automatic report sharing to the seller/dealer are deferred.

## Enquiries, VIN and quote

One service conversation begins as an enquiry, including questions without VIN or purchase. It may convert to one paid order. A second order uses a new request conversation. Unpaid enquiries do not consume provider capacity. An operator may send one helpful reminder after 24 hours waiting for the user's answer/quote choice, never after decline or stop request; then wait for the user. New user messages may continue the request without automatically creating or buying an order.

Collect exact VIN with optional source Listing context. Harmless whitespace/uppercase normalization may assist input, but never silently replace ambiguous characters. Standard VIN-format acceptance is an input check, not proof of authentic physical identity. Applicable provider-supported vehicle/year/market rules and an authorized availability check must precede payment instructions. Distinguish malformed input, unsupported vehicle, no available history, identity mismatch and temporary provider failure. No records does not mean nonexistent/fake vehicle. Operator clarifies uncertain cases and withholds payment instructions.

User may edit VIN before quote acceptance. Later correction requires operator review and renewed confirmation before any purchase. Customer explicitly confirms exact VIN, available-provider offer, 100 TMT price, applicable refund rule and specific scheduled fulfilment day/window before paying. A quote retains those accepted facts and cannot be silently changed by later Listing edits or status/ownership changes. Warn the user to recheck VIN. A customer-confirmed wrong VIN followed by a change to another car requires another purchase after provider purchase; operator buying a different VIN is company error and must be corrected at company cost or refunded if undeliverable.

## Payment and commitments

Payment is external; no in-app checkout or automatic SIM/account access. Real collection and refund methods are activation inputs, not assumed cash/SIM integrations. Users may submit receipt screenshot or sender phone; neither confirms funds automatically. Assigned authorized operator verifies actual receipt. Record claim, verification, amount, actor and reconciliation evidence without exposing payment details to unrelated staff. Duplicate, partial, unmatched or uncertain receipt stays in review and cannot trigger a second purchase.

Payment instructions hold one slot for the accepted scheduled day for two hours. Unpaid holds expire and release atomically. Late receipt requires operator reconciliation and a newly agreed available day or founder refund decision if no acceptable booking exists. It cannot disappear, silently reactivate an expired slot or auto-purchase. Evidence submitted before expiry is not itself verified receipt. Verify/expire races retain the actual received/confirmed timestamps and one authoritative capacity outcome. After-hours receipt remains awaiting verification until 09:00; extending instructions past close must show this notice and cannot guarantee a morning slot from receipt submission alone.

Staffed daily 09:00-20:00 Asia/Ashgabat. Normal available-capacity delivery estimate is one staffed hour. Paid queue order is oldest confirmed payment first; older paid backlog precedes new orders. Unpaid enquiries have a separate response queue. Store timestamps in UTC and calculate these commitments using the named zone.

Book today and at most the next staffed day. Same-day delivery is possible until 20:00 only when assigned staff can meet the accepted promise before close; no universal 19:00 cutoff. Otherwise agree next-day delivery before payment. Prepaid next-day work promises delivery by 10:00 and must be capped to demonstrated achievable morning throughput, even below the stated provider allowance. Provider allowance and staff capacity are separate constraints. If neither day has bookable capacity, allow enquiries but stop payment instructions. If one staffed hour is infeasible, obtain acceptance of a later explicit window before payment. Do not extend routine hours or silently roll commitments over.

Provider allowance/reset/counting is configurable only from verified account evidence for each provider, including other-channel consumption, preflight, invalid/no-data, duplicate, timeout and retry effects. Synthetic capacity is clearly labeled. Never implement 100/day as a verified universal contract merely because the founder stated an allowance.

## Fulfilment, failure and refunds

One operator claims a request; explicit audited handover changes ownership and prevents the old owner from independently fulfilling it. Authorization and claim version are rechecked on payment confirmation, purchase intent, artifact association and delivery. Chat-message idempotency alone does not prevent duplicate payment/provider purchases. Use an order-scoped purchase intent and reconcile uncertain external purchase outcome before retrying. Delivery/reopen never silently reruns the provider.

Before delivery, operator checks confirmed VIN, provider, original date, readable usable PDF with expected pages and matching approved shared link. Store original artifacts/provenance and delivery association. Provider “no data” after payment or inability to supply a usable PDF enters failure recovery and founder-approved refund, rather than automatic substitution of another VIN/provider promise. One available provider is sufficient under the agreed offer; no available provider is not successful fulfilment.

At a missed paid-history promise escalate immediately. One further staffed hour is allowed only if the user agrees to the explicit recovery deadline. Refusal goes straight to founder refund decision; re-missing the recovery deadline also goes to that decision. Record agreement and timestamps. Failed upload/open/download is retried within this recovery policy, not treated as fulfilled merely because a storage key exists. No ordinary customer cancellation after payment confirmation, including before provider purchase; service failure and company error remain distinct refund exceptions. Founder approves refunds. Approval and actual refund execution are separate audited facts; pending/unavailable refund method cannot be presented as completed money return. Customer receives status and explanation.

## Artifacts, archive and personal records

In-app order/chat/payment/report access is purchaser plus specifically authorized staff. Assigned operators see current records needed for their cases; founder controls historical payment-evidence access and bulk exports. Do not grant another paying customer access to an earlier purchaser's conversation, receipt or identity when reusing a licensed artifact. Customers may download/share supplied PDFs and links with anyone where provider rights permit. No product link-revocation promise is required; no permanence promise is inferred. Explain external link expiry/failure and that shared copies may circulate outside Carberk.

Archive every delivered check with exact VIN claim, vehicle identity, provider, original report date, source Listing if any, reported mileage/date where present, original artifacts, rights/provenance and restricted order/delivery association. Attributed operator notes are distinguishable from provider facts. Do not alter original dates or fabricate a combined local inspection score. Archive lookup/reuse respects the actual license; “our own history” does not turn licensed source text into unrestricted company property.

Separate useful vehicle/market observations and genuinely anonymized commercial aggregates from purchaser linkage, contacts, private communications, payment evidence, private diagnostic data and provider restrictions. Retain permitted durable knowledge indefinitely where allowed, otherwise for the longest justified permitted period. Do not apply the rejected blanket 90-day service or 24-month diagnostic purge. Do not infer forever retention of private chats. Record purpose, authority/consent where required, permitted use/reuse/export, identifiability, review/deletion schedule and justified holds for each category. Required missing category policy blocks real collection/activation; software may exercise synthetic fixtures. Account deletion removes/anonymizes personal linkage and removes personal content as required, subject only to documented specific obligations/holds. VIN and reversible pseudonym alone are not anonymous. Existing analytics schedules remain unless explicitly amended; no raw customer sale, actual commercial disclosure or sales portal is authorized.

## Verification and activation

Tests must prove scoped reads/downloads, expired/removed staff, stale claim/handover, atomic quote/capacity/receipt races, two-hour expiry/late payment, FIFO, close-of-day and next-day morning cap, confirmed-VIN immutability, uncertain provider purchase, duplicate fulfilment, one-versus-no provider, PDF failure/recovery/refund, provenance/reuse isolation and category-specific deletion. Database/storage/queue enforcement requires real disposable integration boundaries; fakes cannot supply the protections under test. Render enquiry/customer/operator/refund/history flows in TK/RU/EN, including inaccessible/empty/loading/error states. Focused actual PDF open/download/share/push proof belongs to consolidated campaign acceptance unless an earlier technical choice needs it.

Default live provider/payment operations disabled. Enable only after exact supplier entity/product/account/territory, staff access, resale/archive/reuse/PDF/link/sample/branding permissions, preflight/coverage/quota contract, workable receipt/refund process, measured morning capacity, personal-record schedule/basis/disclosures, named trained staff and verified recovery are recorded. No scraping, unsupported automation, paid report generation, messages or commercial disclosure is authorized merely by this PRD. General Support and diagnostic booking have separate activation gates.
