## Workspace and tasks

Preserve the current authenticated admin top navigation. Add Improvement without replacing reports, inspection-interest history, catalog or audit. Default Overview shows contact intent per time-weighted active inventory, separate Call/Conversation counts, first accepted Messages, matured replies, publication/upload friction, distinct account/guest returns and owned fixes. Journeys & returns exposes current seven-step Sell progression and qualifying buyer/auth/return outcomes. Reliability & fixes shows attributed sanitized failures, release scope, collection freshness and linked fixes.

All admin and analytics workspaces support Turkmen, Russian and English, including navigation, screens, charts, filters, forms, validation, empty/loading/error/denied states and user-facing exports. Locale switching preserves authorized task/filter state and selects localized labels, number/date formats and export headings. Keep stable technical identifiers and original provider artifacts unchanged; identify source language rather than inventing translated originals. This final founder requirement supersedes the earlier Russian-only initial Improvement exception. Verify all three locales and affected layouts in consolidated acceptance. The English prototype is an interaction artifact, not three-locale acceptance evidence.

## Scope and states

Default seven completed UTC days versus previous seven; Today is separate triage. Label Listing city as marketplace context, not physical user location. Show exact filters and explicitly identify independent cohorts or fixed-release investigation fixtures. Preserve filters when opening/closing evidence.

Loading, unavailable, delayed, empty inventory, incomplete observation window, suppressed small group and valid zero are distinct states. Withhold comparisons when collection is incomplete; immature D30 is unavailable, not zero retention. Counts accompany rates and small-sample labels. Apply privacy grouping across filter combinations; no individual inference through subtraction.

## Investigation and fixes

The evidence drawer first shows aggregate evidence and scope. Only founder or explicitly granted investigators can open pseudonymous timelines. Require a short reason and successful audit before detailed reads/scoped exports. Limit exported evidence to the authorized purpose/scope; expose no Message content/media/credentials/URLs. Aggregate viewers cannot access detailed evidence or assign fixes.

Preserve dashboard scroll/filter context, restore keyboard focus on close and provide Escape/focus containment and usable small-screen layout. No new native screens are implied. Link existing GitHub issues with owner/status and verification evidence; do not silently create issues or mark fixes verified from green metrics.
