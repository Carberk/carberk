# Dealer access and economics

> Revised direction, 2026-10-14. Founder chooses free dealer access. This mutable strategy replaces the earlier subscription hypotheses; product capability belongs to [38 — Dealer workspace and showroom](../features/38-showroom.md). Material product changes remain proposed until ADR formalization.

## Current choice

Dealer workspace, staff tools and public showroom access are free. No trial expiry, paid dealer tiers, subscription checkout, paid inventory priority or paid identity badge. Do not build a billing/entitlement engine around the retired tier proposal.

The purpose is useful, accurate inventory and responsive buyer contact. Pilot identifiable businesses with manual approval and personal staff sign-in. Exact recruitment size, business evidence and success thresholds remain open. Free access does not bypass moderation or capacity/abuse rules; these need fair explicit policy rather than payment gates.

## Costs and evidence

Track onboarding/support effort, storage and delivery cost, active available cars, stale inventory, enquiry response and actual dealer retention. Do not treat phone taps as completed sales or invent revenue forecasts. Those observations help size operation and decide which tools are useful.

A diagnostic inspection is a separate physical service with one agreed transparent price to be set before launch. Free dealer access does not imply free inspections, a dealer subscription bundle or preferential inspection results. See [inspection service strategy](inspection-program.md).

## Changes requiring a new decision

Any future subscription, paid feature gate, paid badge or inspection bundle requires explicit founder approval and reconciliation of the owning PRDs/ADR. Ranking remains governed by the vision's ban on pay-for-placement. Do not reactivate the old pricing tables or market-size estimates as commitments.
